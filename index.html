    Story

    The feature film, titled “ARTIST” is a psychological thriller that explores the life of a struggling artist who wants to prove his worth to the world.

    The story begins with the protagonist, an artist, being kidnapped. His family desperately tries to find him. However, the plot takes a twisted turn when it's revealed that he has actually kidnapped a couple and is holding them hostage inside a house for 40 Lent days.

    He takes an oath not to kill them with his own hands, meaning he shouldn't be the direct cause of their death.

    As the story unfolds, the film reveals why he has done such a thing, and whether the couple dies as he intended. The real motive behind his actions is something he is deeply in need of. A personal mission that drives his madness.

    Over time, the protagonist begins to lose his ability to distinguish between reality and his imaginary world, ultimately leading to his tragic death. In the end, he succeeds in proving to himself and to others that he is capable of achieving anything he sets his mind to, but at a devastating cost.

    Act I Set up

    Establish the kidnapping plotline and the search for the protagonist.

    The first act of the film is the setup phase. We are introduced to the protagonist, a budding artist who is struggling to find his place in the world. We also get to know that he has been kidnapped by someone, and his sister is desperately searching for him. This initial setup creates a sense of tension and mystery that hooks the audience and keeps them engaged.

    Act II Confrontation

    Major plot twist reveals the true nature of the protagonist's situation.

    The second act is the confrontation phase. Here, the audience is given a major plot twist when we learn that the protagonist has actually kidnapped a couple, not the other way around. This twist takes the story in a new direction and creates a sense of unpredictability. The protagonist's hostages are kept in two different rooms, and we see how he interacts with them and how he's able to keep them captive for so long. This act also introduces the concept of method acting and the protagonist's obsession with it.

    Act III Resolution

    Revelation of the truth behind the protagonist's actions.

    The final act is the resolution phase. We learn the truth behind the protagonist's actions, as he reveals everything to a psychiatrist. The audience sees the protagonist's descent into madness and his eventual suicide. However, he is able to achieve his goal of proving that he can do anything he sets his mind to, even if it leads to his own destruction. The film ends on a bittersweet note, leaving the audience with a sense of contemplation and reflection.

    The world

    In this world, the artist's perspective is unique and colourful, and they see the world in ways that most people can't even imagine, and it is shaped by their creativity and imagination. It reflects the beauty and complexity of the human experience.

    Through my film, I invite the audience to explore this world of the artist and to experience the unique perspective that only an artist can bring. Whether it is the way they see colour, light, and shadow, or the way they interpret emotions and human behaviour, the world of the artist is a rich and diverse landscape that is sure to impress and inspire.

    As a filmmaker, I have carefully crafted this world to be both accessible and engaging, drawing on my own experiences as an artist to create a world that is both authentic and imaginative. I am confident that this world of the artist will captivate audiences and leave a lasting impression.

    What the true incident is all about

    The true inspiration behind Artist is the tragic passing of Heath Andrew Ledger, the legendary Australian actor known for his unforgettable portrayal of the Joker in “The Dark Knight”. His deep commitment to method acting and the emotional toll it took became the foundation for this story. While Artist is a fictional narrative, it draws from the psychological intensity and haunting reality behind Ledger's experience.

    This connection is intentionally hidden within the story, only revealed in a powerful twist at the very end. I believe that moment will leave audiences stunned, rethinking everything they've seen and emotionally connecting with the deeper message. It's more than a plot twist — it's the film's emotional core and major selling point.

    Director statement

    As a writer-director, I approached this story with the intention to challenge both myself and the audience.

    Inspired by true events, Artist tells a story that many may feel familiar with, yet the way the film unfolds keeps its real story origins hidden until the very end and makes the audience surprised. The film is structured non-linearly, drawing viewers into a puzzle-like narrative that pieces itself together only at the final moment, when the truth is revealed in a jaw-dropping twist that reframes the entire experience.

    With massive visuals and a haunting emotional core, Artist explores themes of obsession, identity, and the weight of societal expectations. My goal is to leave the audience surprised, moved, and provoked to think deeply about the stories we tell ourselves and the roles we play. This is more than a film — it's a reflection of our internal struggles, and the price we pay for chasing greatness.

    — Dani (a) Purushothman, writer & director
